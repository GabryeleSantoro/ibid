# Chain Care

## Replace at 0.5% stretch

Chains do not literally stretch; the pins and rollers wear, so the chain gets longer over time. Measure it with a chain checker tool every month or so. Replace the chain at 0.5% stretch on 11- and 12-speed drivetrains, or at 0.75% on older 8- and 9-speed ones. A new chain costs little compared with the rest of the drivetrain.

## Clean and lube

Clean and lube the chain every 300 km, and after any wet ride. Wipe off the old grime with a rag, use a degreaser if it is really dirty, let it dry, then apply one drop of lube to each roller while turning the cranks backwards. Leave it for a few minutes and wipe off the excess: lube belongs inside the rollers, and any left on the outside just collects grit that grinds the parts down.

## The cassette

Do not ride a worn chain to save money. A worn chain wears the cassette and the chainrings, because the longer chain rides up the teeth and reshapes them. Replace the chain in time and one cassette can outlast three chains. Leave it too long and a new chain will skip on the old cassette, forcing you to replace both.
