# Baking Sourdough

## The dough

My everyday loaf is a 75% hydration dough: 500 g of strong white flour, 375 g of water, 100 g of active starter and 10 g of salt. Mix the flour and water first and leave them for an hour so the gluten starts to form on its own, then add the starter and salt and squeeze them through the dough. Over the next three hours, do four sets of stretch and folds, thirty minutes apart, then let the dough rise until it has grown by about half and feels airy.

## Shaping and proving

Shape the dough into a tight ball, place it seam up in a floured banneton, cover it and leave it in the fridge overnight. The long cold prove develops flavour and makes the loaf easier to score.

## Baking

Preheat the oven with a cast-iron pot inside to 250 °C for at least forty-five minutes. Turn the cold dough onto baking paper, score it with a sharp blade and lower it into the hot pot. Bake at 250 °C covered for 20 minutes, so the trapped steam lets the loaf spring. Then take off the lid, turn down to 230 °C and bake uncovered for 20 minutes until the crust is deep brown. Cool for an hour before cutting.
