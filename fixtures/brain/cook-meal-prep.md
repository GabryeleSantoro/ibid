# Meal Prep for the Week

## Cook in batches

On Sunday I cook grains and proteins in batches. A big pan of brown rice or quinoa, a tray of roasted chicken thighs, a pot of lentils and a tray of roasted vegetables cover most lunches for the week. Cooking everything at once uses the oven efficiently and means one big clean-up instead of seven small ones. Keep the seasoning simple, then vary each meal with a different sauce, dressing or spice mix.

## Storage

Store each component separately in airtight containers so nothing goes soggy and you can mix and match. Glass boxes with clip-on lids last for years and go straight from fridge to microwave. Let hot food cool for no more than an hour before it goes in the fridge, and label each box with the day it was cooked.

## How long it keeps

Most cooked food keeps three to four days in the fridge. That is why I prep twice a week, on Sunday and on Wednesday evening, rather than trying to cover seven days at once. Anything I will not eat by Thursday goes into the freezer on day one. Rice is the one to watch: cool it quickly and eat it within a day or two.
