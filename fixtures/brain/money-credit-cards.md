# Using Credit Cards Well

## Pay the full balance

The single most important habit is to pay the full balance monthly. Set up a direct debit for the full statement amount, not the minimum. If you do, most cards charge no interest at all and you get purchase protection, cashback or points for free. If you do not, card interest rates of 20% or more a year quickly wipe out any reward.

## How interest compounds

Card interest compounds: interest is charged on the unpaid balance and then added to it, so next month you pay interest on the interest. Paying only the minimum on a large balance can take decades and cost more than the original purchases. If you already carry a balance, a 0% balance-transfer card with a clear plan to clear it before the offer ends can stop the snowball.

## Credit utilisation

Lenders look at your credit utilisation, the share of your available credit limit that you are using. Keeping utilisation under 30% on each card, and in total, helps your credit score. Paying the card off before the statement date lowers the reported balance. Do not close old cards you have paid off: their limits and long history both help your score.
